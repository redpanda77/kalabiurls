Kalabi Privacy Policy

Effective: October 2, 2026

Kalabi is designed to work without an account. We do not sell personal information, show advertising, track you across apps or websites, or use advertising SDKs. Kalabi does collect anonymous usage and crash data to improve the app, as described below, and you can turn this off at any time.

Information stored on your device

Your learning progress, review history, custom vocabulary lists, preferences, and related study data are stored locally on your device. Kalabi does not upload this information to us. If you export a backup, you choose where that file is saved or shared.

Anonymous usage and crash data

Unless you turn it off, Kalabi sends anonymous usage events to PostHog (hosted in the United States). These are simple counts such as the app being opened, onboarding being completed, a learning, review, or flashcard session starting or finishing, how you rated a review (again, hard, good, or easy), and a pronunciation being played. Kalabi also sends crash and error reports to Sentry (hosted in the European Union), containing technical details such as the error message, the app version, and the device model and operating system version.

This data is identified only by a random identifier generated by the app. Crash reports carry the same random identifier as usage events so that a problem can be matched to what led to it. It is not linked to your name, email address, or any account, and Kalabi does not send your IP address, advertising identifier, the words or text you enter, your vocabulary lists, or your study content. We use it only to understand how the app is used and to fix problems, and we do not use it for advertising, sell it, or share it with anyone other than PostHog and Sentry, which process it on our behalf.

Network access and external services

Kalabi includes its core lessons, audio, and study data for offline use. If optional content is not available locally, the app may request public character-stroke data from the jsDelivr content delivery network. Links to additional lessons may open kalabi.ai in your browser. These services may receive standard technical request information such as an IP address and browser or device details under their own privacy practices. Kalabi does not attach your learning history, custom lists, name, email address, or advertising identifier to those requests.

Permissions

Kalabi does not require location, contacts, camera, microphone, photo-library, health, or tracking permission for its normal learning features.

Your choices

You can turn off anonymous usage and crash data at any time in Kalabi under Settings, then Privacy, by switching off “Share anonymous usage data.” Once it is off, Kalabi stops sending this data. You can reset learning data from within the app or remove locally stored data by deleting the app. You can also export a backup before doing so.

Children's privacy

Kalabi does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. If you believe personal information has been provided to us, contact us so we can investigate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Kalabi's features or legal obligations change. The effective date above will identify the latest version.

Contact

Questions about Kalabi or this policy can be sent to kalabiapp@protonmail.com.
